Cikarang 17 November 2026 – Strengthening its commitment to professional excellence and high-quality academic service, Adam Kurniawan Library (President University Library) sent its Reference Librarian, Laurensius Yoel Fabian, to participate in the national librarian certification program organized by the National Library of Indonesia (Perpustakaan Nasional Republik Indonesia / Perpusnas). This prestigious competency assessment serves as a benchmark for librarians across the nation to validate their professional expertise and service standards.

  

The certification journey commenced on November 5, 2026, with an intensive briefing and competency reinforcement session held at the Theater Room of Perpusnas Salemba, Central Jakarta. During this preparatory phase, participants were equipped with comprehensive guidelines and technical standards required for the assessment. The program then continued with a formal virtual opening ceremony conducted via Zoom Meeting on November 13, 2026, officially welcoming all participating librarians from various institutions.

  

Following the preparatory stages, the main competency certification assessment took place over two days, from November 16 to November 17, 2026, at the Perpusnas Auditorium. Throughout the evaluation, participants underwent rigorous testing covering core librarianship competencies, including reference services, information literacy, and user-centered academic support.

  

Laurensius Yoel Fabian successfully completed all stages of the certification assessment, demonstrating strong proficiency and readiness in delivering professional reference services. This reflects the continuous dedication of Adam Kurniawan Library’s staff to keeping pace with evolving standards in information management and academic research support.

  

Beyond individual professional development, achieving national librarian certification plays a crucial role in supporting the institutional accreditation of Adam Kurniawan Library. Having certified librarians is a key indicator required to maintain the library's standing as an advanced and competent academic library in accordance with the national standards established by Perpusnas.

  

Through this certification success, Adam Kurniawan Library reaffirms its commitment to continuously enhancing human resource competencies. By investing in certified professionals, the library strives to deliver reliable, innovative, and high-standard academic services for students, lecturers, and researchers across President University.(Laurensius Yoel Fabian)
