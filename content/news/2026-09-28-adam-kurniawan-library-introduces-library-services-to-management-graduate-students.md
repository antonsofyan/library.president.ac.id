Jakarta, 26 September 2026 — Adam Kurniawan Library at President University introduced its library services and resources to students of the Master of Management in Technology and Doctor of Management programs. The session was designed to familiarize graduate students with the library’s facilities, academic resources, and services that can support their learning, research, and academic activities.

The introduction began with an overview of the Adam Kurniawan Library and practical information on how students can access its services. Students were informed about the library’s operating hours and the procedures for visiting the library, including the requirement to complete the guest book upon arrival. The presentation also highlighted the library’s role in providing resources to support students throughout their academic journey.

Participants were also introduced to the library’s Online Public Access Catalog (OPAC), which allows students to search for books by subject, author, or title. The session explained how students can check book availability and obtain materials either independently from the library collection or with assistance from library staff at the circulation desk.

In addition to physical collections, the presentation introduced several services that can support graduate-level study and research. Students learned about the library’s e-resources, including online journal and article databases subscribed to by President University. The library also provides access to Turnitin, which can be used to check papers for plagiarism and AI-generated content, with access organized according to faculty requirements.

The session further covered the discussion room reservation system, giving students information on how to reserve rooms online using their President University email accounts. Students were encouraged to make reservations in advance, while last-minute bookings can also be made directly through the circulation desk, subject to room availability.

Through this introduction, students of the Master of Management in Technology and Doctor of Management programs are expected to become more familiar with the Adam Kurniawan Library and make effective use of its academic resources and services. With access to books, electronic databases, discussion spaces, and academic integrity tools, the library continues to support students in developing their research and academic capabilities throughout their graduate studies. (M. Salman Alfharezi)
