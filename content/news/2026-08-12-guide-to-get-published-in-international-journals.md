**President University, August 10, 2026** – To enhance the quality and volume of scholarly output across the academic community, Adam Kurniawan Library at President University, in collaboration with Emerald Publishing, successfully hosted a webinar titled "Guide to Get Published in International Journals" on Monday, August 10, 2026. The online event, held via Zoom.

The session featured Irwan Sukardi, Business Manager Indonesia at Emerald Publishing, as the keynote speaker. Throughout his presentation, Irwan provided practical insights into the standards and criteria international publishers look for in submitted manuscripts. He emphasized the importance of thoroughly understanding ethical guidelines to make research submissions competitive on a global scale.

A highlight of the discussion centered on the responsible use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in manuscript preparation. Irwan explained that while AI tools can streamline the research process, researchers must maintain academic integrity, data transparency, and clear documentation to preserve the credibility of their scholarly work.

The webinar also clarified co-authorship roles and responsibilities to help researchers avoid authorship conflicts. Furthermore, common ethical issues and publication misconduct were discussed in detail, equipping President University academics with the knowledge needed to safeguard their professional academic track records.

Through initiatives like this, Adam Kurniawan Library aims to remain a strategic partner in fostering a vibrant, high-integrity research culture. Armed with a deeper understanding of publication ethics, President University scholars are better positioned to make meaningful contributions to the global academic community. (Laurensius Yoel Fabian)
