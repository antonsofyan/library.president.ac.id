Cikarang, October 22nd, 2026 — President University Library, in collaboration with Emerald Publishing, successfully held “Exploring Emerald’s Academic Resources” on Thursday, October 22, 2026, at the Auditorium, 5th Floor, Building A, President University. The event featured Irwan Sukardi, Business Manager Indonesia at Emerald Publishing, as the speaker. Organized into three sessions, the program welcomed nearly 300 students from the Management, Accounting and Agribusiness, and Actuarial Science and Business Administration programs.

The session aimed to help students navigate the transition from high school to university-level learning, particularly in developing independent learning, information literacy, and academic research skills. While textbooks remain valuable learning resources, university students are also expected to engage with scholarly journals and other academic publications. These resources provide more specialized and up-to-date perspectives on their respective fields, helping students connect classroom learning with current developments, emerging trends, and real-world issues.

During the sessions, students were introduced to Emerald Publishing’s academic resources and guided through a hands-on demonstration of the platform. The demonstration began with an introduction to the Emerald website, followed by instructions on how to create an account and explore its available content. Students also learned how to navigate the platform and identify resources relevant to their academic needs.

A key part of the demonstration focused on effective searching and filtering techniques. Students were encouraged to use filters to narrow their search results and identify relevant scholarly materials more efficiently. This approach is particularly important when working with a large collection of academic resources, as it helps students save time and focus on information that directly supports their coursework, assignments, and research projects.

The session also introduced students to Emerald’s scholarly journals and case studies. Through journal articles, students can explore research findings, theoretical discussions, and current developments across different disciplines. Meanwhile, case studies offer opportunities to examine real-world situations and apply academic concepts to practical challenges. Together, these resources can help students strengthen their critical thinking, develop evidence-based arguments, and improve the quality of their academic writing.

Throughout the program, several key learning outcomes were emphasized: discovering reliable academic resources, exploring Emerald’s e-journals, improving literature-searching skills, supporting academic writing with credible sources, and building research skills from the beginning of university life. By becoming familiar with these resources early in their studies, students are better prepared to approach academic assignments and research with greater confidence and responsibility.

Through this collaboration with Emerald Publishing, President University Library continues to support the university community in developing information literacy and fostering a strong academic culture. The program reflects the Library’s commitment to helping students move beyond textbook-based learning toward a more research-oriented approach, equipping them with the skills to find, evaluate, and use scholarly information effectively throughout their university journey. (Laurensius Yoel Fabian)
