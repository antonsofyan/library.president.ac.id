Cikarang, August 29, 2026 -- To help new students smoothly navigate their academic journey, Laurensius Yoel Fabian the Reference Librarian from the Adam Kurniawan Library (President University Library) delivered an informative session during the online Pre-University Welcome Night on August 29, 2026. Held via Zoom Meeting, this orientation aimed to introduce new students to the library's vital role as a hub for academic support, learning resources, and research assistance throughout their studies at President University.

During the presentation, Fabian highlighted key operational details, including the library's opening hours and locations. Students were introduced to the Adam Kurniawan Library, situated on the main campus, as well as the dedicated Faculty of Medicine Library, which specifically serves medical students. The session outlined step-by-step procedures for borrowing printed books, loan duration limits, renewal options, and essential library rules and etiquette designed to maintain a quiet, comfortable, and collaborative learning environment for everyone.

In addition to printed collections, Fabian emphasized the extensive digital resources available to all enrolled students. The presentation guided new students on how to access the library's electronic resources—including academic databases, e-journals, and e-books—which are accessible both on and off campus. These digital platforms provide crucial support for coursework, literature reviews, and independent study needs.

Furthermore, the session addressed academic integrity services provided by the library, specifically the Turnitin checking service. Fabian explained how Turnitin assists students in upholding high standards of academic honesty by reviewing their papers for proper citation and preventing unintentional plagiarism before the submission of final assignments.

The interactive Zoom session concluded with a Q&A segment, giving new students the opportunity to ask questions regarding library services, digital access, and membership benefits. Through this welcoming event, the Adam Kurniawan Library reaffirms its commitment to guiding new students toward academic success and fostering a strong culture of research and literacy from their very first day join at President University (Laurensius Yoel Fabian).
