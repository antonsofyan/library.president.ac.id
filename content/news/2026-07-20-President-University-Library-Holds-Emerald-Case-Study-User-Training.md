President University, July 17, 2026 – To support high-quality teaching, learning, and research, President University Library, in collaboration with Emerald Publishing, hosted an insightful virtual session titled "User Training Case Study Emerald." The event took place on Friday morning (17/7) via Zoom Meeting, drawing faculty members, researchers, and students eager to maximize academic resources.

The session featured Irwan Sukardi, Business Manager Indonesia at Emerald Publishing, as the keynote speaker. He opened the training with a comprehensive overview of Emerald Case Studies, emphasizing their vital role in bridging theoretical knowledge with practical real-world applications in modern higher education.

Participants were guided step-by-step through navigating the Emerald platform efficiently. Mr. Sukardi shared advanced search strategies and content filtering techniques designed to help users quickly locate relevant business and management case studies tailored to their specific curriculum and research needs.

Beyond basic navigation, the session delved into the structural nuances and learning objectives embedded within Emerald’s case studies. The speaker outlined best practices for faculty members on how to seamlessly integrate these practical cases into classroom teaching, while also showing students how to utilize them for critical analysis and academic projects.

The interactive webinar included a hands-on demonstration, allowing attendees to practice searching, accessing, and downloading resources in real time. Practical tips were also shared to assist academics in leveraging Emerald’s digital repository for ongoing professional development and institutional output.

The event concluded with an engaging Q&A and open discussion, where participants explored customized use cases and addressed specific user needs. Through initiatives like this, President University Library continues its commitment to enhancing information literacy and providing top-tier academic resources for the entire university community.(Laurensius Yoel Fabian)
