Malang 4-6 November 2026. The 5th Indonesian Academic Library International Conference (KPPTI) was held on November 4–6, 2026, at the Ballroom of Rayz UMM Hotel, Malang, East Java. The Central Board of the Indonesian Academic Library Forum (FPPTI), in collaboration with FPPTI East Java Regional Board and Universitas Muhammadiyah Malang (UMM), organized this event under the theme: "Role Shift in Academic Librarianship as AI Moves Forward: Technology Integration, Competency Upskilling, and Institutional Adaptability".

Neilany Edwina Nulampau, S.Sos., M.Si., Head of the Adam Kurniawan Library, attended the event and representing the President University, took part in the symbolic signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the National Library of the Republic of Indonesia.

The main focus areas of the conference were:

1.  The Shifting Role of Librarians: Transforming academic librarians from mere conventional information managers into strategic partners in knowledge navigation and artificial intelligence (AI)-based learning ecosystems.
    
2.  Technology Integration: Exploring cutting-edge AI-based tools, systems, and methodologies to enhance library services, collection management, and information retrieval.
    
3.  Competency Upskilling: Formulating new competency standards through reskilling and upskilling initiatives related to data literacy and AI literacy.
    
4.  Institutional Adaptability: Strengthening the governance, policies, and organizational strategies of university libraries in sustainably responding to technological disruption.
    

The opening ceremony was attended by:

1.  Principal Secretary of the National Library of the Republic of Indonesia and Chair of the Indonesian Librarians Association (IPI), Dr. Joko Santoso, M.Hum.
    
2.  Head of the National Library of the Republic of Indonesia, Prof. E. Aminudin Aziz, M.A., Ph.D.
    
3.  Vice Rector I of Universitas Muhammadiyah Malang, Prof. Akhsanul In’am, Ph.D.
    
4.  Director General of Science and Technology at the Ministry of Higher Education, Science, and Technology (Kemdiktisaintek), Prof. Ahmad Najib Burhani, M.A., M.Sc., Ph.D.
    
5.  Participants from various provinces, comprising librarians, library managers, academics, and researchers from across Indonesia (including delegations from Papua).
    
6.  International speakers from Singapore and Malaysia.
    

Other activities included:

1.  Signing of Memoranda of Understanding (MoU) and Cooperation Agreements (PKS) between the National Library of the Republic of Indonesia and 45 FPPTI member universities.
    
2.  FPPTI signed Memoranda of Understanding with six partner journal managers, namely:a. Jurnal Media Informasi (Universitas Gadjah Mada)b. Jurnal Pustakaloka (UIN Ponorogo)c. Jurnal Daluang (UIN Walisongo Semarang)d. Jurnal Ilmu Informasi Perpustakaan dan Kearsipan (JIIPK) (Universitas Negeri Padang)e. Jurnal Palimpsest (Universitas Airlangga)f. Indonesian Journal of LibrarianshipThis collaboration opens publication pathways for the best papers presented at the 5th KPPTI.
    
3.  The National Seminar featured three main topics: a. Prof. Imas Maesaroh, M.Lib., Ph.D. (UIN Sunan Ampel) discussed "AI, Open Science, and the Future of Scholarly Communication". b. Setio Basuki, M.T., Ph.D. (Universitas Muhammadiyah Malang) presented "Reimagining the Library Space and Services in the Post-AI Era". c. Ida Fajar Priyanto, Ph.D. (Universitas Gadjah Mada) reviewed "Global Trends in AI-Driven Academic Libraries".
    
4.  Parallel Call for Paper presentations held across four rooms according to the conference sub-themes: a. Artificial intelligence and emerging technologies in academic libraries. b. Developing future competencies and skills for librarians. c. Academic libraries as supporters of research, learning, and scholarly communication. d. Institutional adaptability, governance, and sustainable library transformation.
    
5.  The 10th National Congress (Musyawarah Nasional ke-X). This highest organizational forum accepted the accountability report of the 2023–2026 Central Board delivered by Mariyah, S.Sos., M.Hum. (Universitas Indonesia) as General Chair of FPPTI for the 2023–2026 term, and elected Dr. Purwani Istiana, S.IIP., M.A. (Universitas Gadjah Mada) as the General Chair of FPPTI for the 2026–2029 term.
