President University, July 9, 2026 – To elevate academic research quality and foster a strong culture of academic integrity, the Reference Librarian at President University conducted an interactive information literacy session for students enrolled in the Law Investment course on Thursday (9/7).

During the session, the Reference Librarian introduced students to essential tools for legal research, demonstrating how to effectively navigate specialized electronic databases and retrieve primary legal sources. The presentation focused on equipping future legal professionals with the techniques required to filter high-impact academic literature relevant to investment law and international business regulations.

In addition to database navigation, the training provided hands-on instruction on managing citation tools to streamline the referencing process. Students were guided through proper citation practices to ensure their academic writing adheres to global legal standards and avoids common stylistic pitfalls.

A key highlight of the visit was a comprehensive walkthrough of Turnitin, the university’s primary originality checker. The Reference Librarian explained how students can utilize similarity reports constructively to refine their draft manuscripts, improve paraphrasing skills, and uphold the highest standards of academic honesty in their coursework.

By integrating practical library instruction directly into the classroom, the session successfully bridged the gap between theoretical legal concepts and effective research methodologies. Students left equipped with the critical skills needed to conduct thorough literature reviews and produce rigorous, well-supported legal analyses.

President University Library remains committed to partnering with faculty members across all departments to embed research skills into the curriculum, ensuring that students are fully prepared to meet both academic and professional demands.(Laurensius Yoel Fabian)
