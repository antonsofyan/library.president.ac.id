Cikarang, 31 Oktober 2026 – Adam Kurniawan Library at President University collaborated with Emerald Publishing and iGroup to organize an online session titled "Exploring Emerald’s Academic Resources" on Saturday, October 31, 2026. Held via Zoom Meeting from 09.00 to 10.00 WIB, the webinar aimed to equip the university’s academic community with practical knowledge on accessing and utilizing scholarly databases effectively.

The session featured Irwan Sukardi, Business Manager Indonesia at Emerald Publishing, as the keynote speaker. During his presentation, he introduced participants to Emerald’s extensive collection of academic journals and reliable e-resources designed to support both coursework and research projects.

The webinar was primarily attended by Master's (S2) and Doctoral (S3) students from President University, for whom high-level academic literature is essential. Through the demonstration, postgraduate students learned how to retrieve peer-reviewed journals and high-impact studies directly applicable to strengthening the literature reviews and theoretical frameworks of their theses and dissertations.

Furthermore, the session served as a strategic stepping stone for postgraduate researchers aiming to publish their work internationally. By gaining direct insights from Emerald Publishing, Masters and Doctoral students were equipped with practical knowledge on journal selection and manuscript preparation to support their academic publication goals in Emerald's reputable journals.

Attendees were also guided through essential strategies to improve their research skills, including techniques for searching relevant scholarly information and incorporating academic sources effectively into their writing. These insights helped postgraduate scholars navigate digital literature more efficiently throughout their research journey.

In addition to database navigation, the webinar provided a comprehensive guide to getting published. Irwan Sukardi highlighted crucial aspects of academic integrity and credibility, covering ethical publication guidelines, responsible use of artificial intelligence (AI), and awareness regarding publication misconduct.

Through this collaborative event, Adam Kurniawan Library continues to foster a strong research culture and promote ethical scholarly practices across President University. (Laurensius Yoel Fabian)
