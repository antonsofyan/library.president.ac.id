Cikarang, 16 November 2026 – Adam Kurniawan Library at President University held a HukumOnline Workshop on November 16, 2026. Taking place from 17.00 to 18.30 at the Charles Himawan Auditorium, President University, the event was organized in collaboration with HukumOnline to introduce the academic community to legal database resources and research tools.

The workshop featured Hana Dirda Hutapea, Customer Experience Officer at HukumOnline, as the main speaker. During the session, she guided participants on navigating the HukumOnline platform effectively, demonstrating how students and faculty members can utilize its legal databases, regulatory updates, and analytical articles to support their coursework and academic research.

Participants gained practical insights into conducting accurate legal literature searches and maximizing the features available through the library's subscribed e-resources. The session provided an interactive learning environment where attendees could directly explore the platform and ask questions regarding legal document retrieval.

Through this initiative, Adam Kurniawan Library continues to strengthen information literacy across disciplines and ensure that the President University community can fully optimize its digital collections. (Laurensius Yoel Fabian)
